## Independent content checks and limits

Visible content is checked separately from whole-page scores and extraction.
Native ink checks bind paint spans to their **actual embedded font program,
resource and glyph IDs**. Subset glyph IDs may be renumbered between PDFs; font
names alone cannot establish identity. The earlier name-only 112,702-glyph claim
was withdrawn. No corruption of library font data was demonstrated.

Independent checks cover source words and mathematical glyphs, owning pages,
rectangle clips, picture identity/foreground and selected paint-order interactions.
The portfolio screen examines 218 opaque rectangles and 22 opaque picture paints;
eleven other fills have disjoint bounds. Controlled before/after renders supplement
these checks. Text presence, PDF structure and manifest validity cannot waive a
missing label, word, glyph or picture.

These checks are bounded. Nonrectangular clips, arbitrary masks/blends/occlusion,
stroked text, oblique/CFF/Type 1 or unavailable font programs, reading order and
annotation appearances may remain unknown. Forty-four Type 1 traces remain
unqualified. Native validation tools stay outside the safe managed library.
Linux rendering checks and Windows native-paint checks retain distinct scopes.

**Office application-view calibration is unfinished.** Cached Office PDFs are
bootstrap references. Exported PNG/PDF agreement is not calibration against the
visible application. Unavailable references block dependent conclusions only;
rejected capture/export actions are not retried or relabeled as completed work.

  Normal controls and twelve compressed body/header/footer outcomes have no cuts.
  The cell trace explains the upward baseline but does not establish Word's
  compressed-cell placement. Exact-source Word references are unavailable; cell
  clips and independently qualified small-row guards remain intact.

  is visible). Two authored 1800pt exact rows clip 1,661 glyphs each. A 450pt table in
  a 216pt column has sixteen cuts/fifteen hidden glyphs. Their Word crop/split and
  placement contracts remain unknown; exact height is not minimum height.
- The pinned Word inline-PNG reference also clips 25% of its transformed picture;
  anchored controls retain the full picture. The earlier universal rotation-cut
  defect claim was withdrawn. One fixed-slot footer SVG has no colored pixels in
  either candidate or Word PDF; its application-view visibility remains unknown.
  Conflicting historical table-origin anchors and the original undeclared-font
  balloon comparison also remain unqualified.
- Custom 612×842 Review paper retains approximately 83pt body/footer/balloon
  placement errors. A page-height/double-mapping explanation was contradicted.
  Empty-versus-hidden 26pt marks can accumulate 1.815pt per paragraph and change
  pagination; the correct Word direction remains unqualified.
- General note-row fragmentation, mixed section-start contracts and unplaced or
  merged navigation owners remain constrained. In the 300-label overflow witness,

  fifteen extra candidate operations are wholly offpaper. Overflow is warned.
- Legend geometry and dense/manual chart layouts remain approximate. Do not add
  point-count, font or document-shape rules without independent evidence.
- Small source-pinned origin/ink failures remain failures: Review 0.003479pt against
  0.001pt; cell-A 0.016174pt against 0.01pt; note-A 0.066/0.050pt against 0.01pt;

  note marks, typography and broader combinations retain documented limits.
  Ordinary list formatting is now implemented at source resolution; its new

  Locale-dependent alphabets and custom restart policies remain unqualified.
- Coarse host memory admission is partial, not a comprehensive heap ceiling.
  Safe managed execution, cancellation, meaningful input bounds, bounded caches
