`static-{header,footer}-picture-inherited.docx` reuse the public report's prose,
math, table, note, link and PNG. A short first section defines a default static
story with a page-anchored 20pt picture at (420,24); the final section inherits
that reference. Tests repeat the reference or replace it with a text-only story.
Pictures must survive inheritance and disappear after an explicit replacement.

These are public composition controls, not Office-calibrated visual references
or additional independent acceptance documents.
